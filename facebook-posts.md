## Posting Strategy Tips:

1. **Best Times to Post:** 
   - Weekdays: 9-11 AM, 1-3 PM
   - Weekends: 10 AM-12 PM

2. **Visual Recommendations:**
   - 3D globe screenshots
   - Family lifestyle images
   - Feature highlights
   - Before/after comparisons

3. **Engagement Boosters:**
   - Ask questions in comments
   - Use relevant emojis
   - Include clear CTAs
   - Tag relevant groups (parenting, family, tech)

4. **Hashtag Strategy:**
   - Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
   - Mix popular and niche tags
   - Include branded hashtag (#FamilyBubble)

5. **A/B Testing:**
   - Test different headlines
   - Try with/without emojis
   - Vary post lengths
   - Test different CTAs
