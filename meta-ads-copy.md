## Additional Notes:
- **A/B Testing:** Test different headlines with the same primary text
- **Video Option:** Consider short video ads showing the 3D globe in action
- **Retargeting:** Use Ad 1 (Safety) for retargeting website visitors
- **Lookalike Audiences:** Use Ad 3 (Modern Tech) for lookalike expansion
- **Seasonal:** Ad 2 (Emotional) works well during holidays/travel seasons

## Recommended Ad Formats:
- **Single Image:** Use for Ad 1, 3, 5
- **Carousel:** Use for Ad 4 (show multiple features)
- **Video:** Use for Ad 2 (emotional storytelling)
- **Collection:** Use for Ad 5 (showcase multiple features)
