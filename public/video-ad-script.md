# FamilyBubble Video Advertisement Script

## 30-Second Ad Script

### Scene 1: Opening (0-5 seconds)
- **Visual**: Animated logo appears with pulsing glow effect
- **Text Overlay**: "FamilyBubble"
- **Music**: Upbeat, modern, family-friendly

### Scene 2: Tagline (5-10 seconds)
- **Visual**: Logo fades slightly, tagline appears
- **Text**: "One bubble. One shared space."
- **Voiceover**: "Stay connected with your family, instantly."

### Scene 3: Features (10-20 seconds)
- **Visual**: Three feature icons animate in
  - 👥 Private Family Network
  - 🌍 Real-time Location
  - 💬 Stay Connected
- **Voiceover**: "Share moments, see where family is, and chat in your private bubble."

### Scene 4: Call to Action (20-30 seconds)
- **Visual**: CTA button pulses with gradient
- **Text**: "Get Started Free"
- **Voiceover**: "Download FamilyBubble today. Free to start."
- **Visual**: App logo appears in corner

## 15-Second Short Version

### Quick Cut (0-15 seconds)
- Logo animation (2s)
- Tagline + Features montage (8s)
- CTA button (5s)

## Video Specifications

- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 1080x1920 (Vertical/Stories)
- **Duration**: 30 seconds (full), 15 seconds (short)
- **Format**: MP4, H.264
- **Frame Rate**: 30fps
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (horizontal) or 9:16 (vertical)

## Recording Instructions

1. Open `video-ad.html` in a browser (Chrome recommended)
2. Use full-screen mode (F11)
3. Record using:
   - **OBS Studio** (free, professional)
   - **QuickTime** (Mac)
   - **Windows Game Bar** (Windows + G)
   - **ScreenFlow** (Mac, paid)
4. Record for 30-45 seconds to allow for editing
5. Edit in:
   - **Adobe Premiere Pro**
   - **DaVinci Resolve** (free, professional)
   - **Final Cut Pro** (Mac)
   - **iMovie** (Mac, free)

## Animation Timeline

- **0-1s**: Logo entrance
- **1-2s**: Title fade in
- **2-3s**: Tagline appears
- **3-4s**: Features animate in
- **4-5s**: CTA button appears
- **5-30s**: Continuous loop of animations

## Music Suggestions

- Upbeat, modern electronic
- Family-friendly, positive vibes
- No lyrics (instrumental) or minimal vocals
- 30-second loopable track

## Text Overlays for Video Editing

1. "FamilyBubble" - Bold, gradient text
2. "One bubble. One shared space." - Clean, readable
3. "Private • Secure • Connected" - Feature highlights
4. "Get Started Free" - CTA button text
5. App store badges (iOS/Android) - End frame
