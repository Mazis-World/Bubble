# FamilyBubble demo script

Open familybubble.online. Read this out loud. The bits in brackets are what to tap.

---

This is FamilyBubble.

One bubble. One shared space. A private circle for the people who matter.

[Sign In — email and password]

You’re back in your family space.

This is the radar. Everyone in your bubble shows up as a face. One glance and you can see they’re okay.

[Tap someone]

That’s their profile. How they are, when they last updated, and how far they are.

[Close]

[Update Status]

Status is the heartbeat. An emoji, a short line — “just checking in.” Everyone in the bubble sees it instantly.

[Pick an emoji, type a message, Update Status]

[Map]

Same people, now on the globe. Private to this family. Not a public map.

[Bubbles]

[Invite]

Families grow by invite, not by search. Share a code or a link. When they join, they’re in the same space — they see everyone, and everyone sees them.

[Close]

[Gear]

Photo, role, notifications. You choose what pings you. The bubble stays private.

[Close]
