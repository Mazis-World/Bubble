# FamilyBubble visual demo — walkthrough script

Use this as a presenter script: what to **say**, what to **tap**, and what the audience should **see**.

**Recommended cut:** 6 minutes, sign in to a seeded family, then walk the product.  
**Full first-run cut:** ~10 minutes, create a bubble from Welcome. Both are below.

Live product URL: https://familybubble.online

---

## Before you record or present

Seed this so the globe and radar look full the moment you sign in.

| Role | Name | Status idea | Location idea |
|------|------|-------------|---------------|
| You (owner) | Mom | ☕ `Coffee before school` | Home |
| Child | Emma | 🎒 `At school` | School / city |
| Elder | Grandma | ❤️ `Home` | Home |

1. Use a **phone-width** window (390×844) or a real phone. The UI is built as a mobile PWA.
2. Sign in as **Mom** on the main screen. Keep a second phone or incognito window ready if you will show **Join**.
3. Confirm Mom’s subscription is active (create is paywalled; a lapsed account hits **Your Subscription has Expired**).
4. Turn the camera to **portrait**. Hide bookmarks. Dark room / dark desktop so the glass UI reads.
5. Do not demo SOS, Places, or family memos — those are not in the live app yet.

---

## How to read the beats

Each beat has:

- **Say** — spoken line. Pause after the last sentence so the UI can land.
- **Tap** — exact labels on screen.
- **See** — what should be visible if the demo is healthy.

Skip any *Optional* beat if time is tight.

---

# A. Main demo — Sign In → features (~6:00)

Story: Mom opens FamilyBubble after a busy morning and checks that everyone is okay.

### 0:00 — Welcome

**Tap:** Open familybubble.online. Stay on Welcome. Do not rush the first card.

**See:** Dark glass screen. Cyan–blue–pink logo orb. Title **Welcome to FamilyBubble**. Subtitle **One bubble. One shared space. Instantly connect with your family circle.** Badge **Your Family Galaxy Awaits**. Cards: **Create a New Bubble**, **Join an Existing Bubble**, **Sign In**.

**Say:**  
FamilyBubble is a private family space. Not a social feed. One bubble, one circle — so you can see the people who matter and know they’re okay.

Hold two seconds on the logo.

### 0:25 — Sign In

**Tap:** **Already have an account? Sign In** → Email → Password → **Sign In**.

**See:** Title **Sign In**. Subtitle **Welcome back to your Bubble.** Spinner, then home.

**Say:**  
If you already have a bubble, you just sign in. Email and password. You’re back in your family space.

### 0:45 — Radar home

**See:** Top bar **FamilyBubble**. Toggle **Bubbles** / **Map**. Gear. Radar rings, sweep, **Radar Active**. Face bubbles for Mom, Emma, Grandma. Mom has a **YOU** badge. Bottom bar: **Update Status** and **Invite**.

**Say:**  
This is your family galaxy. Everyone in the bubble is a face on the radar. You, Emma, Grandma — one glance, not a list of chats.

Slowly hover or tap nothing. Let the sweep play.

### 1:10 — A member’s profile

**Tap:** Emma’s bubble.

**See:** **Profile**. Photo, name, role. **Status**. Last updated. Distance if both have locations (**Xm away** / **km away**). Connection level / tier. Close.

**Say:**  
Tap anyone and you get the human version: how they are, when they last checked in, and how far they are — without opening a map first.

**Tap:** Close (X).

### 1:35 — Update Status

**Tap:** **Update Status**.

**See:** Sheet **Update Your Status**. Emoji grid. **Add a quick message (optional)** with placeholder **What’s on your mind?** Location block. Footer: **Everyone in your bubble will see your status and location instantly.**

**Tap:** Pick 😊 or ☕. Type `Just checking in`. Optional **Get My Location**. **Update Status**.

**See:** Sheet closes. Mom’s face on the radar shows the new emoji.

**Say:**  
Status is the heartbeat. An emoji and a short line. Everyone in the bubble sees it instantly. That’s the whole point — not a feed, a pulse.

### 2:10 — Globe / Map

**Tap:** **Map** (globe icon).

**See:** 3D Earth. Family photos sitting on their locations. If someone has no location: **Waiting for Locations**.

**Say:**  
Flip to the globe when you want the where. Same people, now on the Earth. Private to this bubble — not a public map.

Pause. Slowly rotate or use the pause / reset controls if they are visible.

**Tap:** **Bubbles** to return to radar.

### 2:45 — Invite

**Tap:** **Invite**.

**See:** Sheet **Invite Someone to Your Bubble**. Gradient code `BUB…`. Note: when they join they see **everyone**, and everyone sees them. Buttons **Copy Code** and **Share**.

**Say:**  
Families grow by invite, not by search. You share a code or a link. One shared space — not separate chats.

**Tap (optional):** **Copy Code** until it reads **Copied!** Then close the sheet.

### 3:10 — Join, on a second device (optional, +90s)

Only if you have a second phone or incognito window.

**On device 2, tap:** Welcome → **Join an Existing Bubble** → paste `BUB…` → name → photo (skip okay) → **Get My Location** → role (**Son** / **Daughter** / **Grandma**) → email + password → **Join Bubble**.

**On Mom’s screen, see:** A new face appears on the radar. Optional notification if enabled.

**Say:**  
That’s the join. No app store hunt for “the family chat.” Code in, they’re in the bubble, and they show up on the radar.

If you skip this beat, say: *Joining is the same Welcome screen, with an invite code that starts with BUB.*

### 3:20 — Settings & notifications

**Tap:** Gear.

**See:** **Settings & Profile**. Photo, name, role. **Change Photo**. **Edit Profile Details**. **Notifications** (Enable / Status updates / New members / Location updates). **Sign Out** at the bottom — do not tap it.

**Tap:** **Notifications** — point at **Status updates** and **New members**. Do not enable live during a talk unless you want a banner.

**Say:**  
Settings is your photo, your role, and what pings you. Status, new people, location — you choose. The bubble stays private.

**Tap:** Close the sheet.

### 3:50 — Close on radar

**See:** Three faces on the radar. **Radar Active**.

**Say:**  
That’s FamilyBubble. Sign in, see your people, share a status, find them on the globe, invite the next person. One bubble. One shared space. Stay connected to the people who matter.

Hold on radar. End.

# B. First-run demo — Welcome → Create (~10:00)

Use this when the audience has never seen onboarding. Pre-subscribe the email, or be ready for the paywall.

### 0:00 — Welcome (same as A)

**Say:**  
Let’s start a family bubble from scratch.

**Tap:** **Create a New Bubble**.

### 0:20 — Create wizard

Go at a talking pace. **Next** after each line.

| Step | Title | Fill |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | **Name Your Bubble** | `The Millers` |
| 2 | **What’s Your Name?** | First / last for Mom |
| 3 | **Your Email** | Demo account |
| 4 | **Create a Password** | 6+ characters |
| 5 | **Add Your Photo** | Pick a face, or skip |
| 6 | **Set Your Location** | **Get My Location** until **Location Set!** |
| 7 | **Your Role in the Bubble** | **Mom** |
| 8 | **Ready to Create Your Bubble!** | Review, then **Create Bubble** |

**Say, over the wizard:**  
You name the space, you name yourself, you add a face and a place. Role is how the family sees you — Mom, Dad, Grandma, or custom.

### 2:15 — Paywall

**See:** **Where Family Meets Technology.** Badge **Private by design • Built for families.** Grid **Everything Your Family Needs** (unlimited members, location, status, invites, private, notifications). **Monthly** / **Yearly**. **Start Free Trial**. **Restore Purchases**.

**Say:**  
Creating a bubble is Premium. Joining someone else’s bubble is not. The owner starts the space; family comes in on an invite.

**Tap:** **Start Free Trial** (or skip this screen if the account is already subscribed).

### 3:00 — Premium walkthrough

Advance with **Next**. Last card is **Get Started**. You may skip with X.

| Card | Title | Point at |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | **Welcome to FamilyBubble Premium!** | “Let’s take a quick tour of your new family space.” |
| 2 | **Your Family Circle** | Radar — each person is a bubble |
| 3 | **Real-Time Location** | Interactive globe |
| 4 | **Status Updates** | Emojis and a short line |
| 5 | **Invite Family Members** | Code or link |

**Say:**  
Radar. Globe. Status. Invite. That’s the product. Everything else is around those four.

**Tap:** **Get Started**.

### 3:45 — Empty radar, then Invite

**See:** Radar with only **YOU**.

**Say:**  
A new bubble starts with you. The galaxy fills when family joins.

Then run **Invite** and optional **Join** from section A, then **Update Status** and **Map**.

# C. Short 90-second cut

If you only have a minute and a half:

1. Welcome (5s) — “One bubble. One shared space.”
2. Sign In (10s)
3. Radar with three faces (20s) — “Your family galaxy.”
4. Update Status (20s) — emoji + `Just checking in`
5. Map (20s) — three pins on the globe
6. Invite sheet (15s) — `BUB` code
7. Back to radar (10s) — “Stay connected to the people who matter.”

# D. Lines you can reuse

- One bubble. One shared space. Instantly connect with your family circle.
- Your family galaxy awaits.
- Not constantly watching where everyone is — just knowing they’re okay.
- When they join, they see everyone. This is a shared space.
- Everyone in your bubble will see your status and location instantly.
- Private by design. Built for families.
- Stay connected, wherever life takes you.

---

# E. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|--------------|------------|
| **Your Subscription has Expired** | Switch to a subscribed owner account. Do not fight the renew wall live. |
| Radar with one person | Invite was never accepted, or you signed into the wrong bubble. |
| **Waiting for Locations** on the globe | **Update Status** → **Get My Location** on each account before the talk. |
| Join code rejected | Codes start with `BUB`. Generate a fresh one from **Invite**. |
| Photo upload fails | Skip photo; letter avatars still look like the product. |
| Paywall on Create | Expected. Join flow does not show this. |
